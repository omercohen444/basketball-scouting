Scoring rhythm

{%- for cell in runs %}{{ stat(cell) }}{% endfor %}

A scoring drought is three minutes or more of quarter clock with no point of any kind. A field-goal drought is three minutes with no made field goal, so free throws end the first and not the second — a team can look like it kept scoring while going without a basket. Neither is carried across a quarter break. These describe patterns; none of them claims a cause.
